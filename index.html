Why this deal

In February 2025 Saipem and Subsea7 signed a memorandum of understanding to merge — the largest consolidation in European offshore energy services in a decade. The combined company would hold roughly $25 billion in revenue, a $51 billion backlog, and a fleet spanning the full offshore engineering and construction value chain, from shallow-water EPC to ultra-deepwater subsea installation.

My analysis builds standalone valuations for both companies from six years of extracted annual-report data, then models the pro-forma entity: exchange-ratio economics, synergy assumptions tested line by line, accretion / dilution, and the combined credit profile. The historical workbook — every figure traced to its source page — is downloadable below.

I spent four years inside oilfield operations before moving to finance: procurement and cost analysis across a fleet of fifteen rigs. When management claims vessel-utilization synergies, I have an operator's view of which claims survive contact with an actual offshore schedule — and which don't.
